About Charles A. Paris

Charles Anthony Paris, professionally known as Brother OTG, is a record producer, software developer, and author. He is the founder of CHARLES PARIS MUSIC. His production credits include collaborations with artists such as Beanie Sigel and Tone Trump, with his work featured on major networks including BET, MTV, and VH1.

As a voting member of the Grammy Awards' Philadelphia and Chicago Chapters for over a decade, Paris has made significant contributions to the music industry. He also develops educational content and free web-based applications for music producers under the Charles Paris Music Official Site brand.
